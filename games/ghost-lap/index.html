HOW TO BUILD YOUR OWN TRACK ARCHITECT A HIGH-SPEED NEON CIRCUIT IN 4 EASY STEPS
01

SHAPE THE LOOP

Click around the canvas in order (clockwise or counter-clockwise). Ghost Lap links each point into a continuous glowing asphalt ribbon. You need at least 4 nodes to form a closed racing loop.

02

START / FINISH GRID

Node #1 automatically becomes the Start/Finish line (marked in gold). The car spawns facing toward Node #2, so make your first two points a straight launch runway!

03

CHECKPOINT SECTORS

Checkpoints are auto-generated perpendicular to each sector (green gates). Complete all sectors in sequence to log valid lap times and challenge your personal ghost car.

04

TEST & REFINE

Use [UNDO POINT] to correct mistakes without starting over. Click [TEST DRIVE] to drop in and test apex drift lines, then click [SAVE & RACE]!

PRO TIP Avoid crossing track lines over each other. Wide sweeping corners before long straights produce the fastest lap records!
